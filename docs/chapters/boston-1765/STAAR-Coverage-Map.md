# Boston Chapter — STAAR 8 Coverage Map & Content Shift

**Question:** how much of the STAAR Grade 8 Social Studies *assessed* curriculum (TEA, rev. Aug 2024) can the Boston chapter carry?

**Short answer:** Boston is a **Revolution-era** chapter (~1765 Stamp Act → 1775 Lexington & Concord, across Acts 1–4). STAAR 8 spans **all** of U.S. history to 1877 (85 assessed student expectations across 4 reporting categories), so Boston can only own a *slice* — but it can own the **single most valuable slice**: it **fully carries the Revolution-causes Readiness standard (4)(A)** and can give **meaningful coverage to 23 of the 85 SEs (~27%)**, heavily concentrated in the Revolution + founding-principles clusters. Of those 23, only **5 are Boston-only (ONCE)**; the other **18 are spiral standards** Boston *introduces* and later chapters reinforce (see the target set below). With a **light content shift** (below), Boston becomes the definitive vehicle for that whole cluster while *honestly* touching a set of Geography/Culture/Citizenship/Economics standards the Revolution naturally exercises.

It **cannot** (and shouldn't try to) cover colonization-before, the early republic, westward expansion, sectionalism, the Civil War, Reconstruction, 19th-c. reform, or industrialization — those want sibling chapters. This validates the multi-chapter product structure.

Legend: **● Full** (Boston is the natural, primary carrier) · **◐ Partial/thematic** (Boston touches it authentically) · **○ Out of scope** (other chapter) · **⤴ Reach** (possible but risks distorting the history — optional).

## The content shift — how to capture the most, by Act

Boston history *already contains* almost all of this; the shift is mostly **labeling + lightly adding named individuals, a town-meeting beat, quartering, and the Port Act**, then wiring them as carriers/micros so the debrief can assess them. All additions obey the existing rules (macro = path-invariant spine; micro = optional/reactive; interaction = tracking).

**Act 1 — 1765 Stamp Act (built):** already carries (4)(A) core [Stamp, representation, post-war policy], (11)(A)/(10)(C) port, (21)(A) Loyalist POV, (21)(B) press, (15)(C) writs + vice-admiralty grievances, (23)(E) Daughters of Liberty seed. **Add:** a brief **town-meeting** touch (→ 3(A) representative govt) and name **King George III** as the Crown's face on proclamations (→4B).

**Act 2 — 1770 Boston Massacre:** **Add carriers for** — **Crispus Attucks** + the Massacre event (4B, 4A escalation); **quartering** of soldiers billeted in town (→15(C)/3rd-amdt grievance); **John Adams defends the soldiers** (→20(A) civic virtue, 19(C) rule of law/juries, 21(A) POV); **free press / propaganda** via the Massacre engraving (→21(B)); rope-walk **class friction** (→23(B) urbanization). Thread C (the soldier) already humanizes the other side.

**Act 3 — 1773 Tea Party:** **Add carriers for** — **the Boston Tea Party as civil disobedience** (→20(B), the marquee Supporting standard); the **Old South town meeting** that precedes it (→3(A) representative govt); **Samuel Adams** + **committees of correspondence** (→4B, 10(C) communication, already earmarked for this Act); the **tea monopoly / mercantilism** angle (→4A, 12).

**Act 4 — 1774–75 Coercive Acts → war:** **Add carriers for** — the **Intolerable/Coercive Acts** incl. the **Boston Port Act** closing the harbor (→4A, 10(C)/11(A) port geography + economic punishment) and the **Quartering Act** (→15(C)); **Lexington & Concord** (→4C); **Washington's arrival at the Siege of Boston** (→4B, 22(A)); **Mercy Otis Warren / Abigail Adams** as women's-contribution carriers (→23(E)); the **1776 Declaration** setup (→1(B)).

**Cross-Act:** the founding-principles thread — **natural rights / rights of Englishmen** (15(A), 15(E), 19(A)) — runs under every Act as the "why" behind the grievances; surface it in Syncs/debriefs rather than as a separate errand.

## World fit — do we need to expand the town?

**No core expansion needed.** The existing world (`manifest.ts`: a continuous Boston town, wharf in the west → Liberty elm in the east, harbor to the west/southwest) **already contains the real historical sites** for all four Acts. The chapter is *designed* to re-dress the **same map across the years** — which is also what powers the cross-Act threads (the same streets and faces changing 1765→1775). Same-world reuse is a **feature, not a compromise**.

**The Acts' key locations already exist:**

| Historical site (Act) | Real event | Existing asset in the world |
|---|---|---|
| **Custom House / King St** (A2) | the **Boston Massacre** (Mar 1770) | `customs` `bldg-customhouse` @ x55 (+ `townhouse` civic @ x53) |
| **Town House / court** (A2) | **John Adams** defends the soldiers | `townhouse` `bldg-townhouse-civic` @ x53 (reuse interior as the court) |
| **Ropewalk** (A2) | the ropewalk brawl → Massacre spark | `ropewalk` `bldg-warehouse-street` @ x−103 |
| **Griffin's Wharf + ships** (A3) | the **Boston Tea Party** (Dec 1773) | wharf apron + moored `ship-brig-hero`/`ship-sloop` at the deck edge; harbor water (WaterDirector) |
| **Old South Meeting House** (A3) | the **town meeting** before the Tea Party | `church` `church-meetinghouse` @ x71.5 (reuse interior for the meeting) |
| **Patriot tavern** (A1–4) | Sons of Liberty organizing | `tavern` "Bunch of Grapes" @ x−18 |
| **Liberty Tree** (A1–4) | effigy + rallying point | `liberty-elm` @ x95 (pocket) |
| **The harbor** (A4) | the **Boston Port Act** closes the port | same wharf/water, re-dressed as blockaded/empty |
| **Town gates / roads out** (A4) | march to Lexington; the alarm ride | `wharf-gate` @ x−118, `east-gate` @ x80 |

**What each Act actually needs is re-dressing + new actors/props — not new geography:**
- **World turns (re-dressing the same map):** 1765 tense town → **1770 occupied** (soldiers, sentry boxes, the Massacre aftermath, March snow) → **1773 tea crates on the wharf** → **1774–75 closed port + barricades**.
- **New character rigs (tint/archetype where possible):** redcoat soldiers (A2+), and the assessed individuals — **Crispus Attucks, Samuel Adams, John Adams, Mercy Otis Warren, Abigail Adams, George Washington** (finale cameo). King George III stays offscreen (portrait/proclamations).
- **New props/textures:** tea crates, sentry boxes, barricades/blockade, Port Act notice, Massacre-aftermath dressing, extra posters. All standard asset-pipeline work; **no map expansion.**

**The one true geographic gap: Lexington & Concord (and the Siege of Boston).** These are **11–18 miles outside Boston** and are **not** rebuilt into the town.

**Decision (locked): a small gated country-road corridor** east of the town gate — a narrow linear "chute" blocked by a diegetic barrier until Act 4 (1775), terminating in a compact **Lexington Green + Concord North Bridge** pocket, with Washington's arrival at the Siege framed from the same edge. It obeys the route-gating law and the harbor-exclusion rule (extends *east*, away from the open water). This is an appendage, **not** a town expansion.
- **Build-ready geometry, bounds extension, blocker, and asset list:** `World-Design-Bible.md` → *"Eastward expansion — the Lexington/Concord country road (A4 gated corridor)."*
- **Whole-curriculum context:** `Curriculum-World-Map.md` (Boston + 4 settings covering all 85 SEs).

**Bottom line:** the core town is sufficient for ~all of the assessable Boston content. Budget goes to **re-dressing + ~6 new character rigs + a handful of props**, plus **the one small gated Lexington/Concord corridor.**
